BRING YOUR OWN POLICY

Bring your own policy

Training runs separately. Each model receives the state and returns a legal move. Assign different policies to Red and Black.

Available models

  1. 1. Copy the template

    Copy models/team_template to models/your_model. Add your weights and give model.json a unique ID and name.

  2. 2. Implement your policy

    Load weights once in Policy.__init__. Return a legal move or action ID from select_move. Point python at your training environment if it needs PyTorch.

  3. 3. Check, select and play

    Reload models, run Check integration, then assign the model to Red or Black. The check uses three sample positions without changing your match.

The random baseline and templates are untrained. They test integration, not playing strength. This app does not train your model.

See MODEL_API.md for the contract and README.md for setup and research rules.

Research rules and scope

Supports standard piece movement, horse legs, elephant eyes, cannon screens, check and facing kings. Checkmate and stalemate both lose.

Research draws: three occurrences of a position including side to move, or 120 plies without capture. Competition perpetual-check/chase adjudication is not implemented.

The experiment limit truncates an episode; it is not a draw. Observations include counters and repetition history needed by these rules.

Learning on both sides forms a turn-based Markov game. A fixed opponent can support a single-agent MDP. Full observability alone does not establish the Markov property.
